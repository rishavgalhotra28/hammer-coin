The strategy

The order is the product. The game comes first, so the first holders have played. The sales come second, at three rising USDT prices. The market opens at the same price as the public round, so the pool does not undercut the buyers.

How the token works

HMR is a BEP-20 token with 18 decimals. The contract does not mint more. You either earn it, or you buy it in a round. Spending it happens inside the game and, later, in votes.

What it is for

  1. Play. Missions, matches, and tournaments pay HMR.
  2. Trade items. Skins, upgrades, and power-ups on the in-game market.
  3. Stake. Lock HMR for bonus rewards and VIP access. Emissions run for 36 months and decline over time.
  4. Vote. Holders vote on updates, features, and treasury proposals once governance opens.

How a player receives it

  1. Register a wallet in the game. MetaMask or Trust Wallet on BNB Smart Chain.
  2. Play. Points build from missions, wins, the leaderboard, and invites.
  3. Connect the same wallet and claim. The player pays the network fee, in BNB.
  4. 20% of the claim can move that day. The other 80% vests over 3 months.

One wallet per verified account. A minimum amount of play is required. The top earners can be reviewed by hand.

The HMR/USDT market

The liquidity bucket is 3.6 crore HMR. Only 60 lakh goes into the first pool, because that is what 150,000 USDT can price at 0.025. The other 3 crore waits until there is matching USDT. The opening liquidity receipt is locked for 12 to 24 months.

The plan, in order

Dates are the working plan from the project roadmap. They move if the game, the audit, or the sale moves.

  1. Q3 2026
    The game opens the airdrop Hammer runs in beta on Android, iOS, and web. Players connect a wallet, earn points, and invite others. The contract is deployed on the BSC testnet. The site and the community channels are live.
  2. Q4 2026
    The token exists, and the early sales open HMR deploys on BSC mainnet after an audit. Claims open on the vest. Seed and pre-sale run. The HMR/USDT pool is created and the liquidity receipt is locked.
  3. Q1 2027
    Public round at 0.025 USDT The public sale, a first exchange listing, staking, the in-game market, and a tournament season with HMR prizes.
  4. Q2 2027
    Holders get a vote Governance, a bridge toward Ethereum and Polygon, hammer collectibles, and a second version of the mobile game.
  5. Late 2027
    A wider game economy A virtual arena, guilds, HMR payments in partner games, and an esports circuit. A burn on fees is part of this phase, not part of the opening supply.
